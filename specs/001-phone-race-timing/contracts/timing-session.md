# Local Device Contract

The pilot exchanges JSON over HTTPS on an operator-enabled desk listener bound to the local network interface. The configured name or IP must match the desk certificate and the browser must trust its chain. Do not bypass certificate warnings or serve pilot data or pairing over plaintext. Exchange a short-lived, single-use pairing code for a device credential scoped to the active desk session. Send credentials outside URLs and exclude them from logs. Invalidate them when the desk session ends. The simulator invokes the equivalent operations in process.

| Operation | Request | Response and rules |
|---|---|---|
| Pair | Short-lived operator code and device label | A session-scoped device credential. The operator sets roles and lanes. |
| Read heat | Credential | Heat and attempt IDs, state, assigned lanes and swimmer or team labels, capture mode, and starter authority. |
| Clock exchange | Exchange ID and phone clock epoch | Exchange ID, desk epoch, and desk receive and send timestamps. The phone records its own send and receive timestamps. |
| Report sample | Exchange ID and phone timestamps | The desk uses its saved exchange timestamps and returns an estimate ID or asks for a fresh sample. |
| Request phone start | Attempt ID, unique request ID, clock epoch and estimate ID | Recheck starter authority and readiness through the grant deadline plus the expected maximum duration for phone-timed lanes. With only stopwatch lanes, require the phone starter’s clock-error bound to be at most 10 ms through the grant deadline. Reserve the attempt and issue a one-use grant with a desk-clock deadline or reject the request. Retries return the original outcome. |
| Report start outcome | Attempt, grant and event IDs, capture timestamp if captured, and audio outcome | Report timed playback with evidence sufficient to bound audible onset, definite no playback, or uncertain. A late grant response cannot trigger audio. The phone uses its clock uncertainty and calibrated audio allowance before playback. Retrying delivery reuses the same event. The desktop alternative records its start locally. |
| Submit lane result | Attempt and event IDs, lane, and either captured timestamp plus estimate ID or stopwatch duration | Require the assigned device and mode. Stopwatch entry can proceed with expired sync. An authorized exact retry returns the original acknowledgement while the desk session is active. A new submission for an Applied attempt returns Attempt closed. Conflicts leave the first accepted result intact. |

Reject invalid credentials, stale attempts, mismatched assignments, conflicting event IDs or values, and invalid time payloads. Hold a finish received ahead of its start for the same armed attempt. Compute elapsed time after accepting the start. Evaluate the clock-error bounds of the actual estimates at each capture and flag a sum over 10 ms or a finish outside the covered window. A changed estimate or epoch must pass the same result check. With a missing start, let the desk review the capture and apply only results with valid durations.

The starter reads the selected signal mode and horn duration from the desk for both preview and the armed heat. In countdown mode the phone requests the grant after the three short tones, shortly before the scheduled long horn. Only the long horn has a start timestamp. A late or refused grant suppresses the long horn.

While a grant is pending, the desk blocks competing starts, assignment changes and rearming. A definite no-playback outcome may release the reservation as a failed start. If playback may have begun and evidence is missing, late, or insufficient to bound onset by the desk deadline, enter **Start uncertain** and require operator resolution before rearming. Expiry without a report also enters Start uncertain. A late report may establish a valid start for the same unresolved attempt if its original capture was inside the grant window and its evidence bounds onset by the deadline. Capture time or successful audio scheduling alone is insufficient. Confirm the playback evidence and its onset bound against physical audio in the pilot.

After the deadline, the desk offers **Confirm existing start** only when the operator witnessed the horn for this attempt and the desk has a capture from the authorized starter and clock epoch whose uncertainty interval fits inside the grant window. Confirmation keeps the original timestamp and all submissions for that attempt. Captured lane results retain the start-uncertainty flag and require explicit approval before Apply. **Discard attempt** closes the attempt without applying its results. A fresh attempt has a new ID and empty submissions. Operator resolution and incoming start reports are serialized so a late report cannot reopen a discarded attempt or replace an operator-confirmed start.

Keep submissions in a same-tab retry queue before presenting them as captured. Show **Pending**, **Accepted**, or **Rejected** for each submission. Restore queued payloads on reload and retry them after reconnect with their original event ID, attempt ID, clock epoch and estimate ID. Resume or reload may require a new estimate for future captures. The desk retains the mapping for captures made in an earlier epoch of the same attempt. A retry from that epoch uses its original mapping. Retrying a start outcome never plays the horn. Reload restores only delivery state. A grant from the prior page cannot authorize playback in the new page.

A successful Apply closes the attempt to new submissions. Serialize submissions with Apply so arrivals are admitted before the successful close or visibly rejected after it. A failed Apply leaves the attempt open. Keep original acknowledgements by attempt and submitting device until the desk session ends. A retry does not reopen review or alter the competition.

The desk shows the active heat, assignments, readiness, and timeouts. Review lists the lane, swimmer or team, time source and duration or missing status. The operator confirms changes to existing times before applying the reviewed values.
